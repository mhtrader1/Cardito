FAQ

Which blockchains do you support?

Cardito is multi-chain from the start. Choose Sonic, BNB, Polygon, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, or Avalanche — more to come.

How do XP and leaderboards work?

Earn XP by playing matches, keeping a daily streak, and inviting friends. Compete on the Global (all-time XP) and Weekly leaderboards. From Season 2, XP unlocks custom room creation; you can also hold XP for future conversion to CARDITO.

How can I earn crypto?

Players will be able to host custom rooms and tournaments with real prizes starting in Season 2. CARDITO token holders will share in platform revenue by the final season.
