FAQ

Do I need a crypto wallet to start?

Yes. An empty crypto wallet is enough. Just sign the registration message—no transaction or fees—and you’re connected. Your in-game progress is tied to your wallet.

Which blockchains are supported?

Cardito is multi-chain from day one: Sonic, BNB, Polygon, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, and Avalanche — with more networks to come.

How does XP work?

Earn XP by playing matches, keeping your daily streak, and inviting friends. XP helps you climb leaderboards and unlock future benefits such as creating custom rooms or converting XP to CARDITO.

When is launch?

Season 1 launches October 2025. Follow us on Telegram and X for updates.
