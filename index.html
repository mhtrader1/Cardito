FAQ

Do I need a crypto wallet to start?

Yes. An empty crypto wallet is enough. Just sign the registration message—no transaction or fees—and you’re connected.
Note: your gameplay activity and in-game assets are tied to the wallet you connect with.

Which blockchains do you support?

Cardito is multi-chain from the start. Choose Sonic, BNB, Polygon, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, or Avalanche — more to come.

How can I earn XP?

Earn XP by playing matches, keeping a daily streak, and inviting friends.

When is launch?

We’re targeting October 2025 for the Season 1 milestone. Follow us on Telegram and X for updates.
